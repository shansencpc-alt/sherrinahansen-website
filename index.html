The Person Behind the Pages

Hi. I'm Sherrina.

I'm an author, artist, and creator. I create from the middle of a messy life. Not a perfect one. A real one. I write fiction that lingers. I make art that plays. I believe the things that nearly break us are the same things that make us worth reading about.

I started drawing in the blank pages of coloring books. Not the printed ones. Those already had someone else's lines. I wanted the empty pages. That instinct never left me, and it shows up in everything I make.

Faith keeps me grounded. Family keeps me laughing. Stories keep me honest.
